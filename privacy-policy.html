MyPic Puzzle Game

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

MyPic Puzzle Game ("the app", "we") is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens with your information. The short version: your photos and your profile stay on your phone.

Your photos stay on your device

When you choose one of your own photos, the app copies it into its own private folder on your phone so you can play it again from Your Gallery. Photos are never uploaded, sent or shared anywhere by the app.

The app uses the Android Photo Picker, so it only ever sees the photos you pick. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library, your camera or your files.

You can delete any saved photo in Your Gallery, or all of them at once in Settings → Delete all my photos. Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved.

If you tap Share after a win, the app makes a picture of your result (your photo, name, avatar and score) on your phone and opens the share menu of your phone. It is only sent to the app or person you choose there; the app itself uploads nothing.

Your profile stays on your device

The name and avatar you choose are saved only on your phone, so the app can greet you and show them on your results. They are never uploaded. You can change them at any time in Settings → Edit Profile.

No accounts, no servers

There are no accounts, no sign-in and no online servers. Your progress (levels, stars, play time for the calendar, settings) is stored only on your phone. We cannot see it, and we cannot restore it if you uninstall the app.

Settings → Reset progress clears your levels, stars, calendar and streak on this phone.

Notifications are local

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your phone by the app itself. No server is involved and no data is sent. You can turn it off in Settings or in your phone settings at any time.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: short videos you can choose to watch for a reward (a hint, more time, bonus stars), and now and then a full-screen ad between puzzles. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and usage data (for example which ads were shown) to show ads and measure them. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first; you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy options.

Google's own privacy policy explains how Google uses that data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your photos and profile are never shared with any advertising partner.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and on its public web page, with a new "last updated" date.
